What a file must look like

{# The canonical column order is presentation; the source of truth is the frozenset contract in glasshouse.imports (kept in step by the template tests). #}

Trades: the first line must be exactly

book,trade,trade_date,counterparty,market,direction,quantity,price,delivery_start,delivery_end

Prices: the first line must be exactly

market,as_of,version,period_start,price

Dates look like 2026-06-30. Times need an offset and whole hours, like 2026-07-01T12:00:00+00:00. Direction is buy or sell. Quantity is MW and price is per MWh, in the currency of the market.
